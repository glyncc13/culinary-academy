# Culinary Academy — expanded internal curriculum

Draft for chef and food safety review. Not accredited. Practical tasks require a suitable supervised kitchen; follow current workplace food safety procedures and ingredient specifications. Theory pass mark 80%; practical competence requires separate observation, not currently implemented in the app.

## Level 1 — Food safety and personal hygiene

### Personal hygiene and illness reporting
- Learning objective: Identify when to report illness and avoid food handling.
- Practical exercise: Demonstrate handwashing and identify clean protective clothing; discuss reporting with supervisor.
- Evidence: recipe or task record, observations, relevant safety checks, learner reflection.
- Assessment: supervisor observes safe preparation, method, quality and clean-down; learner explains decisions and corrective actions.

## Assessment plan
Level 1: supervised basic preparation and two simple dishes. Level 2: coordinated two-course production with allergen documentation. Level 3: costed menu and simulated service leadership. For each, require safe working, accurate records, technique, product quality and reflection. Any critical safety failure requires remediation and reassessment regardless of theory score. Use private question banks; no answer keys in this document. Suggested theory blueprint: 20 questions per level, balanced across modules, 80% pass. Current engine and practical evidence workflow require further development before this blueprint is enforced.
